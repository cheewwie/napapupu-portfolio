Two kinds of quote

Research is evidence · feedback is a verdict

Anatomy

Four cues separate them, so none of them has to carry it alone: the mark, the attribution, the label, and where in the chapter they sit. Colour is the weakest of the four and does the least.

A · From research — the users’ voice

Mark
A hanging quotation mark in faint green, outside the text column. No bar, no fill — it is raw material, not a highlight.
Attribution
Anonymised: participant code, the segment that makes the quote meaningful, and the method. Never a name.
Where it sits
Directly under the finding it supports, before the argument moves on. Two or three together is normal.
Size
19px, body colour — one step up from the prose so it reads as evidence, not decoration.

B · Feedback — colleagues on my work

Mark
The bar in the project’s key colour — what the live case study already does, so nothing changes here.
Attribution
Role and organisation, or a name when they have agreed to it. Whoever said it is the point.
Where it sits
After a result, closing the thought it judges. One at a time, never clustered.
Size
21px, headline colour — the loudest quote on the page, because it is the one a hiring reader is looking for.
